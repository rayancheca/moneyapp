### 6a. Formats (HIGH confidence, three independent implementations agree)

**CSV — "Download Account Activity → Comma Delimited". NO header row. 5 quoted positional fields:**

`Date (MM/DD/YYYY) | Amount (signed, debits NEGATIVE, may carry thousands commas) | "*" (literal) | Check Number (usually empty) | Description`

Corroborated by `unixpickle/pecunia` (`importer.go:65-105` — reads every record with no header skip, asserts exactly 5 columns), `seanlewis08/budget_app` (docstring: *"Expected format (NO HEADERS)"*, and `.replace(",", "")` proving thousands separators occur), `openaccountants` (names field 2 as the literal `*`), plus two more. **There is no running-balance column** — the search hits claiming one are all **PDF→CSV converter vendors describing their own output**; do not design against them.

**PDF statement** (read from a real extracted WF consumer statement, `Kiri23/fast-api-nlp/fargoText.html`): `Statement period activity summary` (beginning/ending balance) · `Transaction history` with columns `Date | Check Number | Description | Deposits/Additions | Withdrawals/Subtractions | Ending daily balance` · a closing **`Totals`** row (a free per-file checksum nothing in this repo does today). ### 6b. Code plan
