What the encodings mean

Position — vertical order is net position, biggest net source at the top. Side — right of the spine is money going down the ladder, left is money coming back. Thickness — dollars. Dot cadence — transfer count. Hue — the sending account. Nothing is decorative.

Why net matters here

Four of your six accounts round-trip money. Gross counts a dollar every time it crosses a boundary, so a dollar that goes Chase → Savings → Chase counts twice. Flip to Net and the left-hand arcs collapse — what you are watching disappear is the churn.

Reconciliation gap — verify before shipping

The detector reports 650 transfer groups, but the 13 account-pair edges only account for 516. 134 groups (~21%) are unattributed here — likely legs whose counterparty account is outside these six, or groups with more than two legs. The real service must reconcile to 650 or explicitly show the remainder.
