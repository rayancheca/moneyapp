What changed and why

Read this first

You asked for A, with B's accounts section restyled into A, the spatial charts that came out of C, and the transfer flow. That is what this sheet is. A is not one contributor among four here — it is the law: every colour, every typeface, every rule and every shadow below belongs to A's contract, and B and C contributed structure and interaction only.

The base · Direction A

Unchanged: the masthead, the hero figure that rolls under the crosshair, the line of the year, where it went, the ledger, the letterpress relief and the ink-settling motion. Both token blocks are byte-identical to A's, and this page adds no custom property of its own.

The accounts · B's structure, A's clothes

B's dense table survives whole — account over institution, an inline thirty-day sparkline, the balance, the Δ. B's dark terminal chrome does not: the set is drawn the way A draws a ledger, in hairline rules. It replaces A's “Ten accounts, set in relief”, which is exactly the swap you asked for.

The spatial pair · C's projection, in SVG

C's orthographic projector is kept; C's void is not. SVG, not WebGL — and that is an advantage, not a compromise: it is deterministic, so the app's 243 Playwright snapshots still hold; axe can read it; it costs nothing in the bundle; and every figure carries a table lens and a printed list. Orthographic means no perspective divide, so a height stays proportional to its value at every angle.

The transfer flow · added

The one slice of your money with no home anywhere else, because the Sankey excludes transfers on purpose. Gross says $329,913.57 moved between your own accounts; $95,482.26 of it came straight back. Three pairs run both ways. The Net ⇄ Gross switch is the whole view.

It is one app, not four parts

Click a line in the accounts set and the ledger filters to it. Hover a category in the relief and its ledger entries light up. Activate an arc on the transfer spine and both legs land in the ledger. Every one of those states is also written out in words, so nothing is carried by dimming alone.

Measured, and modelled

Measured: net worth $110,617.82, assets $112,032.51, owed $1,414.69, 9,753 entries, and every transfer edge and count.

Modelled for this mockup, and labelled where it shows: the daily and weekly balance series, the thirty-day sparklines, the month each transfer landed in, and the ledger entries. All of it from fixed seeds — no Math.random, and no clock read: the one date is a fixed UTC instant, so this sheet draws the same pixels every time, in every timezone.

                Why the left side matters

                Three pairs run both ways. A dollar that goes Chase → Savings → Chase crosses two boundaries, so gross counts it twice. Flip to Net and the left-hand arcs collapse — what disappears is the churn, $95,482.26 of it.

                Width and ticks say different things

                SoFi Savings → SoFi Checking is 299 transfers of about $327. Chase → SoFi Savings is 23 transfers of about $3,435. Nearly the same total, opposite behaviour — so dollars are the width and count is the tick cadence. One channel could not say both.

                What is measured, what is modelled

                Every edge total and every transfer count is measured. The month each transfer landed in is not in the brief, so the monthly split is generated from a fixed seed — each edge's months sum back to its real total and its real count. Any window other than All is stamped modelled.

                Still zero token churn across all four merged parts. Every existing OKLCH value is untouched; the four additive tokens (--ink-display, --surface-leaf, --accent-ink, --annotation) are ≥5.4:1 on every surface in both themes, and the three fragments' duplicated copies of this contract were deleted, not merged.

                Account colour is a law

                One account, one hue, on every figure: Chase Checking cyan, SoFi Savings teal, SoFi Checking blue, Robinhood green, Robinhood Cash lime, Capital One indigo, Discover red, Chase Sapphire pink, Venture X orange. Nothing anywhere depends on hue alone — every figure states its numbers in a list or a table too.
