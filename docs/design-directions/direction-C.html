What to try.
  • Front → Quarter. The default camera is dead-on orthographic — at Front the field is the 2D chart you already have. 3D is what happens when you move, not what you land on.
  • Hover a ribbon. Everything else drops to x-ray, the exact balance for the day under your cursor appears in the panel. You never read a number off the geometry.
  • Look below the plane. The three credit cards extrude downward. Debt is not a red colour, it is a hole.
  • Lens → Chart. Watch the terrain flatten and stack. Lens → Table gives the same numbers as real DOM.
  • Toggle ◑ for the colour-vision-safe gain/loss pair, ☾ for dark. Resize the window below 900px to see the phone fallback take over.

The 3D here is hand-written SVG with a real orthographic projector — no libraries, no network. In the shipping app this geometry runs on react-three-fiber; this SVG version is the documented fallback path.
